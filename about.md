## My Story

I've been creating art since around the age of three. I work in a number of styles from funny cartoons to detailed realism. My preferred painting mediums are gouache and acrylic. For graphical design work I use a Wacom MobileStudio Pro 13 with Adobe Photoshop.

I have long been fascinated by birds and fish. Along with being a qualified bird ringer for the British bird ringing scheme, I am also a dedicated aquarist and enjoy catching and observing wild fish.

My early childhood days were spent watching, feeding and drawing the wild birds in my local area under the mentorship of my late Godfather. My home was never without goldfish, which is still one of my favourite species to keep today, and my obsession with them paved the way to many other fish-related activities in adulthood.

My life is dominated by a passion for birds and fish. It is from this passion that the name "Wing & Fin Designs" was selected as a suitable signature for my artwork.

Art and the great outdoors are only part of my story; I also have a long-standing career in software engineering. Despite this I try to keep screen-time to a minimum. I do not use a smartphone.

I am lucky to live in the beautiful Lower Derwent Valley NNR of North Yorkshire which boasts year-round mild weather and flooded "ings" in winter. The majority of my work as a bird ringer is done within the valley, the data of which is important on a national level.

## About this Site

This site was built for the purpose of sharing my world, connecting with others who share similar interests and preserving a unique slice of humanity, hopefully until the end of time.

### What will you find here?

Paintings, sketches, writing and cartoons inspired by the natural world. *This includes fantasy artwork.* A great deal of my work is "anthropomorphised" which means applying human attributes to animals. As a person who studied animal behaviour in college, this is something I do naturally; I read animals by their body language and the environmental context they're within and try to understand them from my own perspective. In other words, I am very good at putting myself in the shoes of animals!

I always think that not only are birds, fish and people all vertebrates but that we are also all "space dust". Since energy is more or less infinite, this makes it entirely possible that our atoms crossed paths at some point in the lifetime of the universe. In short, we are all the same. We're made of the same elements and we may even have common ancestral matter.
This is where I feel that art, science and spirituality start to cross over a bit. Am I a spiritual person? Perhaps, but please don't mistake that for "religious" which I am not.

At times it might feel like there are multiple artists working on this site. I paint, sketch, write and doodle with varying styles. My written thoughts can be scientific, reflective, philosophical or spiritual. Sometimes my art is beautiful, sometimes it is diagrammatic and sometimes it is downright bizarre.
