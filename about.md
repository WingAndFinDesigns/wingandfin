I'm the artist behind Wing & Fin Designs. I've been creating works of art since around the age of three. I work in a number of styles, from silly cartoons to detailed realism. My preferred painting mediums are gouache and acrylic. For graphical design work I use a Wacom MobileStudio Pro 13 with Adobe Photoshop.

I have long been fascinated by birds and fish. Along with being a qualified bird ringer for the British bird ringing scheme, I am also a dedicated aquarist and enjoy catching and observing wild fish.

My early childhood days were spent watching, feeding and drawing the wild birds in my local area under the mentorship of my late Godfather. My home was never without goldfish, which is still one of my favourite species to keep today, and my obsession with them paved the way to many other fish-related activities in adulthood.

My life is dominated by a passion for birds and fish. It is from this passion that the name "Wing & Fin Designs" was selected as a suitable signature for my artwork.

Birds, fish and fantasy creatures feature heavily in my art, with scenes and backgrounds inspired by the local environment. Art and the great outdoors are only part of my story; I also have a long-standing career in software engineering. Despite this I try to keep screen-time to a minimum outside of my working hours. I do not use a smartphone.

<img class="flourish lightbox-ignore" src="{{ '/assets/images/flourish.png' | relative_url }}" alt="">

I am lucky to live in the beautiful Lower Derwent Valley NNR of North Yorkshire which boasts year-round mild weather and flooded "ings" in winter. The majority of my work as a bird ringer is done within the valley, the data of which is important on a national level.
