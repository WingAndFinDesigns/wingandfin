There's a room in my house that could only have been described as a "junk room", which was used for storing all sorts of random things in the most inefficient way possible.\\
It had been this way for quite some time too... but recently while reading the book [Atomic Habits](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits) by James Clear, I had a revelation; this was the perfect room for turning into a full and proper art studio.

<!--more-->

Some of you might be asking "Wait a sec! I thought dedicated art rooms didn't work for you?!"\\
Yes it's true, at several points in my life they did not work for me. I didn't like being locked away and having to make art alone. To be honest this might still be the case, but there is a much worse danger to my art than working alone, and that is trying to work in a distracting environment!

Ultimately, a living room is for relaxing in, right? My living room contains a big a TV, a real fire, beautiful fish tanks and nice company. Why would I ever want to concentrate and work hard in a place that I associate with relaxing on a comfy sofa? It made sense for me to create an organised and productive space for myself.

So over the weekend we ripped out the old carpet and put in a new white/grey vinyl floor which looks bright and neutral. The junk is gone too, not from the house but from the room. Sorting through what to sell and get rid of will be another job...\\
I've already started to move some of my art supplies in but I need a few more storage solutions to make it perfect.

I'm looking forward to this! It's a step in the direction of taking my art and myself more seriously again. I hope it leads to some focus time and productivity going forward. It's true that this time of year often brings artistic hopes and dreams, but I think a dedicated art studio will be beneficial in many ways.

Of course I'll be sharing photos when it's done! Thanks for reading.
